# VOSS Finance: the owners' quick guide

Open https://voss-business.pages.dev/ and sign in with your approved Google account. Choose Finance.

## Add a sale from Instagram, WhatsApp or in person

1. Select **Add a sale**. Website checkout orders already appear automatically; do not enter them again.
2. Choose the **article**, then its **colour**, and enter the number of bags. The saved catalogue price fills in automatically. Change it only if the actual agreed price differed.
3. If you gave a discount, choose **Yes** and enter the total rupee discount for the whole order. The form calculates the amount the customer pays for the bags.
4. Select **Next: delivery & costs**. Choose the order's actual stage and whether the customer payment has reached VOSS. Customer delivery charges and VOSS's courier bill are separate amounts.
5. Enter the purchase cost of one bag, then costs specific to this order. Leave unknown costs blank. Do not add a bulk packaging or advertising bill again on each sale. Tick costs checked only after the bag cost and all other costs are accounted for.
6. Select **Next: review sale**. Check the article, colour, quantity, discount, total, costs and payment stage. Only **Save this sale** creates the record.
7. Later, open **Sales & expenses**, find the sale and edit its status or payment. New sales created through this form retain their original price and discount snapshot. Older records without this information keep their detailed editor so historical prices are not guessed.

## Add packaging, marketing or another bill

1. Under **What would you like to record?**, select Packaging, Ads & marketing, Delivery, Product purchases, Content creation, Tools & subscriptions or Other costs.
2. Describe what you bought and enter the total amount, expense date, who paid, and whether payment is complete.
3. Select **Save expense**. The bill appears under its category in the selected month's spending table. Use **View or edit your saved expense entries** to correct a bill.

## Find the original sheet

Select **View imported sheet**. This opens all dates and filters to the original imported records. The preserved import contains **26 entries with an original total of Rs 64,514.10**. Open a row's detailed editor to see its source row. Date warnings should be reviewed against the original sheet; changing a reporting month does not delete entries.

## See a month's costs and profit

Select the **Month** above the table. **Spending by category** shows product purchases, packaging, delivery, marketing, content, tools and other costs. **Download spending sheet** exports the selected period to CSV. Sales & expenses also has a detailed record export; Reports retains the existing reports.

- Total costs include recorded unpaid bills; they do not mean every bill has been paid.
- Product subtotal = quantity × price per bag − total order discount.
- Customer amount due = product subtotal + delivery charged to the customer.
- Recognised sales income requires both delivery and collection. New orders and uncollected COD are not earned revenue.
- Bag purchase spending includes stock still on the shelf. Profit deducts the cost of bags sold, rather than subtracting the entire stock purchase a second time.
- Returned/refused orders retain their actual outward courier, return courier, COD and other losses. A returned bag's reusable stock value is not automatically treated as destroyed.
- Missing costs keep profit marked incomplete. Never invent a cost just to remove a warning.
